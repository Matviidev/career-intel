Staff Platform Engineer — Northwind Logistics

About the role
Northwind moves 40,000 shipments a day across Europe. We're hiring a Staff Platform Engineer to own our internal developer platform: the paved road our 120 engineers use to build, deploy and operate services.

What you will do

- Own the Kubernetes platform (EKS) and the golden-path templates teams use to ship services
- Design our infrastructure-as-code strategy with Terraform and drive adoption across teams
- Set reliability standards: SLOs, on-call practices, incident reviews
- Partner with product teams to remove friction from their delivery pipeline
- Mentor senior engineers and lead cross-team technical initiatives

Requirements

- 8+ years of experience in backend, infrastructure or platform engineering
- Deep, hands-on experience running Kubernetes in production
- Strong Terraform experience at scale
- Proficiency in Go or TypeScript
- Experience operating PostgreSQL on AWS
- A track record of leading technical initiatives across multiple teams

Nice to have

- Kafka or other event streaming platforms
- Service mesh experience (Istio, Linkerd)
- Prometheus and Grafana
